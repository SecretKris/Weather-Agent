      Stratosphere and the polar vortex

      In winter a ring of fast westerly wind circles the Arctic, 20 to 50 km up. When it breaks down in a sudden stratospheric warming, cold tends to reach Europe three to six weeks later. This is the early-warning layer behind the weeks-ahead outlook.

      Wave 1 amplitude at 10 hPa
